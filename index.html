our story

popped today, not last year

Molly's Popcorn started in 2026 right here in Jaipur, with one cart and a simple idea: popcorn should taste like it was made for you today, not packaged, shipped, and left sitting on a shelf for months before it reaches you.

Every batch is small, popped fresh, and sold directly from the cart. No mass production, no long shelf life, no mystery ingredients you can't pronounce. Just warm popcorn and honest cookies, made the way a neighbourhood snack should be made.

"Every batch is made fresh. No six months on the shelf.
Popcorn that is still warm when it gets to you."
